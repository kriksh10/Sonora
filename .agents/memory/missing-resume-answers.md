description: Expected answer behavior when a question is not covered by the candidate resume.

Questions outside resume coverage must receive experienced, practical answers, not merely brief general-knowledge definitions. Respect the selected answer length. Personal-experience answers must not invent candidate facts; use an approach or clearly hypothetical example when facts are unavailable.

**Why:** The user clarified: "I want it to give experienced answer not just a brief answer."

**How to apply:** Do not treat resume coverage as an eligibility gate for technical or conceptual answers. Preserve the earlier exclusion of a separate resume-grounding enhancement; this requirement repairs existing answer behavior rather than adding that feature.
