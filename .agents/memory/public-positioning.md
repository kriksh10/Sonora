---
name: Public positioning
description: The owner's direction for Sonora's public website messaging.
---

Position Sonora as an AI meeting assistant rather than an interview-only assistant. Interviews remain one use case alongside sales calls, client meetings, standups, and lectures. Keep Sonora's own branding; Cluely is positioning inspiration only, not a source of copied wording, design, or images.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested broader meeting-focused positioning and truthful claims about existing capabilities.

**How to apply:** For public copy, describe saved transcripts rather than automatic meeting notes, summaries, or action items unless those capabilities are independently established. Do not imply screen-share exclusion is guaranteed across every operating system or sharing application.
